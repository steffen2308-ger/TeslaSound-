# Audio credits (CC0)
The build attempts to retrieve publicly playable previews of the following real recordings, process short steady-state loops and mix them with original procedural combustion audio. Previews may be unavailable; the UI reports whether a recording is present for the selected engine.

- Formula 1: Ears68, https://freesound.org/people/Ears68/sounds/181187/
- V8: overmedium, https://freesound.org/people/overmedium/sounds/651534/
- Diesel: Mihacappy, https://freesound.org/people/Mihacappy/sounds/815853/
- Petrol: seth-m, https://freesound.org/people/seth-m/sounds/269771/
- Two-stroke Trabant: blukotek, https://freesound.org/people/blukotek/sounds/412818/

All listed sources indicate CC0. No claim of exact vehicle replication. No sound files are sent from a phone to a server. No AI training.

## Trabant 601 source candidate (CC BY 4.0)
Boštjan Troha / Technical Museum of Slovenia, Sounds Of Changes, TRABANT 601 – TRAMP – engine running, recorded July 23, 2019. https://www.soundsofchanges.eu/sound/trabant-601-tramp-engine-running/ — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . If available, the audio is excerpted, filtered, resampled, normalized and looped. Fallback candidate: blukotek, two-stroke Trabant engine 02, CC0. Only recordings actually included are identified by engines/sample-report.json.

## Horn sounds
The horn samples in app/src/main/assets/horns were generated with ElevenLabs Sound Effects (text-to-sound) for this app. They are not recordings of the named vehicles.
